🚪 Door Opening Procedures

Front Doors

  • Standard: Use door popper tool on post
  • Power Locks: Check for battery power
  • Child Safety: May prevent rear door opening
  • Hinges: Front doors typically hinge rearward

Rear Doors

  • Standard: Use door popper on latch mechanism
  • Child Locks: Check for child safety locks
  • Hinges: Rear doors typically hinge forward
  • Access: May require front door removal first

Hatchback/Trunk

  • Power Release: Check trunk release button
  • Emergency Access: Use trunk through pass-through
  • Hydraulic: Use spreaders for jammed mechanisms
  • Safety: Check for trunk entrapment sensors

🚛 Towing & Lift Points

Front Tow Points

  • Frame Rails: Primary attachment points
  • Engine Cradle: Heavy-duty lifting point
  • Avoid: Suspension components, radiator
  • Capacity: Check vehicle GVWR

Rear Tow Points

  • Rear Axle: Differential housing
  • Frame Rails: Rear section attachment
  • Avoid: Fuel tank, exhaust system
  • Recovery: Use recovery straps, not chains

Lift Points

  • Jack Points: Designated body lift points
  • Frame Rails: For heavy lifting
  • Engine Bay: Engine cradle for front lift
  • Safety: Ensure vehicle stability
